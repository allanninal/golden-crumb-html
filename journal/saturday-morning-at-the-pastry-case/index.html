Saturday morning at the pastry case

On Saturdays we open the doors at 7:00 AM with the usual breads and pastries already in the case. The specialty pastry drop comes out at 8, which is when the Kouign-Amann, a Breton pastry with a candied sugar crust, goes on the shelf. We make only 12 a day.

The case turns over quickly, so if there is something you have your heart set on, come early. The Seasonal Danish changes with whatever fruit we have, and the counter team will tell you today's flavour. Jalapeño Cheddar Sourdough is a weekend special and tends to go first.

We close at 3:00 PM on Saturdays, earlier if the case is empty. For a larger order, a custom cake or a standing loaf, call us during the week and we will plan it with you.
